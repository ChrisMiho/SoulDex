Click to open. Right-click for options.
SoulDex commands:
SoulDex /souldex - open or close the SoulDex
SoulDex /souldex options - popup, sound, announcement and window switches
SoulDex /souldex find <text> - look a soul up by name
SoulDex /souldex add <name> - follow a friend's dex by name
SoulDex /souldex remove <name> - stop following them
SoulDex /souldex scale <60-160> - the window's size in percent
SoulDex /souldex reset - put the window back in the middle, at normal size
SoulDex /souldex test - show a sample popup
SoulDex /souldex version - the version you are running
SoulDex /dex works everywhere in place of /souldex
