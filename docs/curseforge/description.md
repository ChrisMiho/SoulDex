## Their souls. Your collection.

**Hunt. Drain. Claim.** You were going to farm Soul Shards anyway. **SoulDex** makes
every one a trophy: each shard that lands in your bags claims the mob or player it came
from for your dex. Score points, climb the warlock ranks from Initiate to Lord of Souls,
and outrank the other warlocks in your guild.

Made for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Type `/dex` to open it, bind it to a key, or
pick it from the addon list by the minimap.

---

### ◆ The hunt: every kill a trophy

Drain Soul or Shadowburn anything that moves and its soul is yours for good, filed by
zone and dungeon. Elites, rares and bosses show in their colours. Click a soul for its
trophy card: level and type, a one-line epitaph, its mastery stars, how often you
claimed it, and when and where you took it first.

Dungeon souls get their true names too. Where the game hides a name, SoulDex reads it
through a hidden whisper to yourself: nobody else sees a thing. (A boss drained mid-fight
still goes down as Unknown.)

![Mobs tab](MOBS_IMAGE_URL)

### ◆ The raids: drain every one

Every creature in every vanilla raid, from Zul'Gurub to Naxxramas. Unclaimed souls stay
`? ? ?`, bosses are marked before you claim them, and each raid has a progress bar. It
is the one hunt you can finish.

Forever's own raids, **The Barrow Deeps** and **Hyjal Summit**, are listed too. Their
rosters are still sealed; every soul you claim inside is kept for when they open.

![Raids tab](RAIDS_IMAGE_URL)

### ◆ The trophies: every shard has a name

A popup and a sound for every new soul, coloured by what you took: elite, rare, boss or
player, with the points it earned. Mastery stars and rank-ups get popups of their own.
Hover a Soul Shard and its tooltip names the soul inside, so you know exactly whose soul
you're about to spend. The name follows its shard through your bags, the bank and relogs.

![Popups and shard tooltips](TOOLTIP_IMAGE_URL)

### ◆ The rivals: hunt players, outrank friends

Every player you shard is a trophy, sorted by class. SoulDex users in your party, raid
and guild join your leaderboard on their own; add anyone else with `/dex add <name>`.
Browse a rival's dex, read-only: what they have and you don't is highlighted.

![Players and Friends tabs](FRIENDS_IMAGE_URL)

### ◆ The climb: rise through the ranks

- **Points** for each first claim: 1 for a normal mob, 5 for an elite, 10 for a rare
  or a player, 25 for a boss.
- **Mastery stars** for the mobs you farm: 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 750 and 1,000
  claims, then a star every 500 more.
- **Bosses score every time.** Players and rares pay a little again, once a day each,
  so nobody farms a friend.
- **8 warlock ranks**, each with its own emblem: Initiate, Acolyte, Soul Thief,
  Shardbinder, Reaper, Soul Harvester, Soul Tyrant and Lord of Souls.
- **19 named milestones**, from First Harvest to Raid Reaper, each with a progress bar.
- The **Me** tab: your rank crest and your records. Most-claimed mob, nemesis, hunting
  ground, rarest trophy, latest claim, best day.

Your guild hears only the big moments (Reaper and up, the bigger milestones, raid
clears), never every soul. One tick turns it off.

![Me and Milestones tabs](ME_IMAGE_URL)

### ◆ The grimoire: bend it to your will

Every switch has its own tick: the popup, the sound, guild announcements, claim chatter
in chat and the login line. **SIZE** scales the window and the popups from 60% to 160%.
The settings sit behind the gear on the window and as a page under **Settings > AddOns >
SoulDex**. Bind a key under **Keybindings > AddOns**.

![Settings and commands](SETTINGS_IMAGE_URL)

### Commands

`/dex` is short for `/souldex`; every command works with either. `/dex help` lists them
in game.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/dex` | Open or close the window |
| `/dex options` | The settings (also the gear on the window) |
| `/dex find <text>` | Look a soul up by name |
| `/dex add <name>` | Follow a friend's dex by name |
| `/dex remove <name>` | Stop following them |
| `/dex scale <60-160>` | The window's size in percent |
| `/dex reset` | The window back in the middle, at normal size |
| `/dex test` | Show a sample popup |
| `/dex version` | The version you are running |

### How claiming works on WoW: Forever

Forever hides the combat log from addons, so SoulDex reads your target when you start
**Drain Soul** (or cast **Shadowburn**) and names the next new shard after it. When the
game won't give up a name, the soul counts as an **Unknown soul** in that zone (no
points, no popup). SoulDex never guesses: a shard it can't match is left unnamed rather
than recorded under the wrong name. Shards from before you installed it stay unnamed.

*Experimental, off by default:* "Boss names after the fight (test)" names a boss drained
mid-fight once the fight ends, if you keep it targeted.

Raid creature lists are based on Classic Era data and may differ slightly from Forever.
